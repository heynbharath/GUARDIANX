# Glossary

- **Older adult:** person whose safety support is being configured.
- **Caregiver:** authorised family member or support person with an active relationship.
- **Trusted contact:** person who has agreed to receive a configured contact attempt.
- **Device heartbeat:** message indicating the device or gateway is currently communicating.
- **Stale:** telemetry is older than the configured freshness threshold.
- **Disconnected:** system has enough evidence that the device/gateway is offline; do not use when status is merely unknown.
- **Potential fall:** candidate event requiring workflow handling; not a confirmed diagnosis.
- **Inactivity candidate:** period with less activity than a configured pattern; may be caused by routine or missing data.
- **Escalation:** policy-driven sequence of attempts to reach people who can check on the older adult.
- **Acknowledgement:** an authorised person accepts responsibility for follow-up.
- **Resolution:** follow-up outcome is recorded; distinct from acknowledgement.
- **Idempotency:** repeated processing of the same request does not create duplicate effects.
- **Audit event:** append-only record of a meaningful state change or action.
- **Demo mode:** isolated environment using simulated data and fake providers only.
