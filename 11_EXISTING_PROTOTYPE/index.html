Beyond the wrist

Detection is the beginning.
Response is the promise.

Most safety experiences put the burden on someone to notice, understand, and act. GuardianX explores a more connected model—designed around the older adult, their family, and the people who can help.

01 / SENSE
⌁

Passive safety signals

Use supported wearable sensors and movement patterns to identify potential falls, unusual inactivity, or other events that may need attention.

02 / UNDERSTAND
◌

Context-aware risk

Combine sensor signals with configurable thresholds and contextual checks to help reduce unnecessary alerts and prioritise what matters.

03 / CONNECT
⌂

One family view

Give authorised family members and guardians a clear view of status, recent activity, alert history, and the next recommended action.

04 / ESCALATE
↗

Coordinated response

Design an escalation pathway for check-ins, trusted contacts, and—when properly integrated—medical or emergency services.

05 / RESPECT
♡

Low-effort by design

Keep the everyday experience simple for older adults, with minimal interaction and clear, accessible ways to request help when needed.

06 / LEARN
⌁

Built to evolve

A modular concept that can grow from a faculty demonstration into validated hardware, secure data services, and tested response integrations.

From signal to support

A connected safety loop, not another notification.

The experience is designed to connect the moment a potential risk is detected with the people who can respond. Each stage has a clear purpose, with human oversight where it matters.

01

Sense the change

Wearable sensors provide movement and other supported signals. Hardware capabilities depend on the selected device.

02

Check the context

Rules or validated models assess the event and, where appropriate, prompt a check-in before escalation.

03

Reach the right people

Notify designated contacts and guide the next step using configured escalation rules.

04

Track what happened

Keep an auditable event history so the family can see alert status and follow-up actions.

Built around people

Technology should feel
like peace of mind.

The product philosophy keeps the experience grounded in dignity, trust, accessibility, and transparent escalation—not surveillance for its own sake.

PRINCIPLE 01

Independent, not isolated

Support older adults in continuing their routines with confidence, without making them responsible for constantly operating an app.

PRINCIPLE 02

Family clarity over noise

Make status and next steps understandable. Alerts should be prioritised, actionable, and designed to minimise false alarms.

PRINCIPLE 03

Human-centred escalation

Safety-critical actions need clear consent, reliable connectivity, verified contacts, and a carefully tested fallback process.

Project & academic context

A safer future for ageing, built with care.

GuardianX is an early-stage product concept exploring how wearable sensing and coordinated escalation could help families support older adults living independently. This interface is a faculty-demonstration prototype and a foundation for future hardware and software development.
